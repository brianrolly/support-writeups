Working method

A repeatable support cycle

The same structure appears across the cases because it keeps technical work explainable, reversible, and testable.

01 · Establish

Capture the symptom

Record the failure, affected path, scope, timestamps, and known-good state before changing anything.

02 · Isolate

Follow the evidence

Use service state, logs, process data, network observations, and configuration to separate cause from correlation.

03 · Change

Keep rollback intact

Back up, validate candidates, stage risky changes, and preserve a clear route back to the previous state.

04 · Prove

Test the real workflow

Verify services and metrics, then repeat the operation that originally failed instead of stopping at “service active.”
